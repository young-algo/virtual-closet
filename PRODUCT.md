# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **Primary User**: Kevin Turner (Personal Use).
- **Context**: Accessing the app on desktop or mobile before vacations or when organizing his wardrobe collection.
- **Job to be Done**: Easily inventory clothes, edit details, and pack clothes into a vacation checklist with a smooth, efficient interface.

## Product Purpose
- To provide a lightweight, local-first wardrobe explorer and suitcase packing tool.
- Focuses on simple organization, easy drag-and-drop uploads, background isolation, auto-tagging, and exportable packing lists.

## Brand Personality
- **Sleek Utility**: Highly functional, fast, and structured.
- **Minimal Elegance**: Clean light-mode default aesthetic, generous whitespace, refined typography, and minimal decoration.
- **Organic & Human**: Feels like a custom boutique tool rather than a generic SaaS website.

## Anti-references
- **SaaS Website Clichés**: Avoid landing-page eyebrows, side-stripe card borders, generic dark-tech glows, and over-designed SaaS metric layouts.
- **Forced Dark Mode**: Do not enforce dark-mode-only designs; respect preference for light mode default.

## Design Principles
- **Respect User Content**: Let the clean studio-lit closet garment photos dominate the visual interface.
- **Sleek Utility First**: Every action should be fast, discoverable, and requiring minimum clicks.
- **Confidence via Safeguards**: Destructive actions (like clearing progress) must have confirmation blocks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear focus outlines and keyboard navigable forms.
- High-contrast typography matching WCAG AA standard against clean light-mode backgrounds.
